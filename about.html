ABOUT LEVY MARTIN

More Than Menswear.

WHO WE ARE

Designed for Angola. Inspired by Africa. Ready for the World.

Levy Martin is an independent menswear brand created for a new generation. Built in Africa with an international vision, the brand combines timeless elegance, contemporary design and everyday functionality. Every collection reflects confidence, authenticity and purposeful simplicity.

Our products accompany young men through school, friendships, sport, university, personal growth and their future careers.

OUR SIGNATURE

The LM Monogram

The Levy Martin symbol is built from the initials L and M. Its clean geometric construction represents clarity, confidence, craftsmanship and timeless luxury. Designed to become instantly recognizable, it reflects the brand's ambition to build a lasting identity.

Brand Story

Why Levy Martin Exists

Levy Martin was born from a simple observation: many young men struggle to find products that genuinely reflect who they are and who they aspire to become.

Rather than following trends, Levy Martin creates timeless, contemporary products that accompany young men through school, friendships, sport, university and their future professional lives.
