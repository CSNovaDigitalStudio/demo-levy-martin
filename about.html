About Levy Martin

More Than
Menswear.

Contemporary menswear designed in Angola, inspired by Africa and created for the next generation.

Levy Martin combines timeless design, everyday functionality and purposeful simplicity to create clothing that supports confidence, identity and authentic expression.

Our Story

Designed for Angola.
Inspired by Africa.
Ready for the World.

Levy Martin was created for young men who seek more than clothing. The brand exists to create pieces that feel relevant throughout everyday life while expressing ambition, confidence and individuality.
